If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will handle the shipping. For large shipments, the secretary invites three eco-friendly logistic companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles to submit their offers, ensuring that the selection process considers their sustainability practices. In cases of small shipments, the secretary opts for a carbon-neutral postal service. A digital package label is generated and sent via email to minimize paper use, while the logistics department head assesses the need for parcel insurance electronically.

In parallel, warehouse staff prepare the goods for packaging using recyclable materials, ensuring that any waste generated during this process is sorted for recycling. The packaging process is optimized to reduce material usage and avoid production waste by reusing materials whenever possible.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are scheduled for pickup by the selected eco-friendly logistics company through a digital platform, allowing for real-time tracking and minimizing energy consumption. This entire process is documented electronically to streamline operations and reduce reliance on physical paperwork, contributing to a more sustainable workflow.
