If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will handle the shipping. For large shipments, the secretary invites three logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles to submit offers. She selects one based on their sustainability practices and commitment to reducing carbon footprints. In cases of small shipments, normal post shipment is employed, with the secretary generating a package label digitally and sending it via email to the logistics department head. Parcel insurance is arranged electronically if deemed necessary.

In the meantime, warehouse personnel prepare the goods for packaging using recyclable or biodegradable materials to minimize waste. They ensure that any excess packaging material is recycled according to established guidelines.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for pickup by the selected logistics company, ensuring that all processes are optimized for energy efficiency and that renewable energy sources are utilized wherever possible in the logistics operations. All communications and documentation related to this process are managed digitally to reduce paper usage and streamline operations.
