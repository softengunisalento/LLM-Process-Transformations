If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping, prioritizing logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles. For large shipments, the secretary invites three eco-friendly logistic companies to submit offers, ensuring they follow sustainable practices. She selects one based on their carbon footprint and energy efficiency. In the case of small amounts, a digital shipping label is generated and sent electronically to minimize paper use. The logistics department head assesses the need for parcel insurance digitally, opting for providers that support green initiatives.

Meanwhile, warehousemen package the goods using recyclable materials and ensure that any waste produced during packaging is recycled appropriately. They also utilize renewable energy sources for any machinery involved in the packaging process to reduce energy consumption.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for pickup by the selected logistic company, ensuring that all communications are conducted via email or digital platforms to eliminate unnecessary paper usage and streamline the process. This approach not only enhances efficiency but also aligns with sustainable practices throughout the shipping process.
