If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping while prioritizing logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles. For large shipments, the secretary invites three eco-friendly logistic companies to submit digital offers, emphasizing their sustainability practices. She selects one based on both cost and environmental impact. In cases of small shipments, a digital label is generated and sent electronically to minimize paper use, with parcel insurance arranged by the logistics department head if necessary.

Warehousemen can begin packaging the goods using recyclable materials and ensuring minimal waste. They are encouraged to implement practices that reduce energy consumption, such as using energy-efficient tools and machinery. In addition, all packaging materials are sourced from renewable resources wherever possible.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for pickup by the selected logistics company, which is required to report on their carbon emissions for transparency. The entire process is documented digitally to avoid physical paperwork and facilitate easy tracking of sustainability metrics. This digital approach also allows for better communication among departments, reducing delays and enhancing efficiency while maintaining a focus on green software solutions throughout the workflow.
