If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will handle the shipping. For large quantities, special shipping will be necessary. In these cases, the secretary invites three logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles to submit offers, ensuring they prioritize sustainability practices. The secretary selects one of them based on their commitment to reducing carbon footprints and using renewable energy sources.

For small amounts, normal postal shipment is used, with an emphasis on choosing services that implement green logistics practices. A package label is generated digitally by the secretary using a software application to minimize paper use, and parcel insurance is arranged electronically by the logistics department head if necessary.

In the meantime, warehouse personnel prepare the goods for packaging using recyclable materials to minimize waste. The packaging process incorporates eco-friendly materials and practices to ensure that any waste generated can be recycled effectively.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for pickup by the selected logistics company. The pickup schedule is communicated through digital channels to streamline coordination and reduce unnecessary travel. This entire process emphasizes sustainability at each step, focusing on minimizing energy use and waste while promoting environmentally friendly practices throughout the shipping process.
