If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping, prioritizing logistics partners that operate electric or low-emission vehicles. For large amounts, the secretary invites three sustainable logistic companies to submit offers via email to reduce paper waste and selects one of them based on their sustainability practices. In these cases, the process includes assessing the environmental impact of shipping options, opting for any with carbon offset programs. For small amounts, the normal post shipment is used, but only if the service provider employs low-emission logistics. The secretary generates a digital package label using software that minimizes energy use and ensures the logistics department head reviews the need for parcel insurance electronically. The warehousemen can begin packaging the goods with recyclable materials, ensuring that waste is minimized and that any unsuitable materials are disposed of in an environmentally-friendly manner. If any packaging waste is generated, it will be sorted for recycling rather than landfilled. The goods are then made ready for pick-up in an area that maximizes the efficiency of the loading process, reducing energy consumption. Once everything is prepared, the packaged goods’ pick-up is coordinated digitally with the logistics company to streamline communication and avoid unnecessary travel. By doing so, the overall process reduces carbon footprint, energy use, and production waste, while promoting sustainable practices throughout.
