If goods shall be shipped, the secretary first verifies the shipping method by consulting an automated logistics management system that prioritizes eco-friendly options. For shipments with large volumes, the system identifies logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles and sends out a digital invitation for offers. The secretary reviews the proposals electronically and selects the most sustainable option available. In cases of small shipments, the system recommends utilizing a local, eco-conscious courier service that employs renewable energy sources for delivery. The secretary generates a digital package label and, if necessary, the logistics department head handles parcel insurance through an electronic platform to minimize paper usage. Meanwhile, the warehouse team packages the goods using recycled or biodegradable materials, ensuring that waste materials are minimized and properly recycled. Once everything is prepared digitally and packaged sustainably, the goods are set aside in a designated area for pickup by the chosen logistics company, optimized for the lowest carbon footprint based on the selected shipping method.
