If goods shall be shipped, the secretary utilizes a digital platform to confirm the shipping method and carrier, ensuring a focus on logistics companies that prioritize electric or low-emission vehicles. For large shipments, she invites three eco-friendly logistics companies to submit their offers via email, assessing their sustainability practices before selecting one. In the case of small shipments, the system automatically generates a digital package label to minimize paper usage, and the logistics department head reviews any necessary parcel insurance digitally to avoid physical document handling.

During this time, the warehousemen prepare the goods for packaging, ensuring that all materials used are recyclable and sourced from renewable resources. They utilize waste materials from previous shipments for cushioning and securing the products, enhancing waste reduction and recycling efforts.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are logged into a digital tracking system that alerts the chosen logistics company for pickup. The system also monitors the carbon footprint associated with the selected carrier, ensuring decisions are made with sustainability in mind. Throughout this process, all communications and documentation remain digital to minimize energy use and reduce the need for physical resources. This streamlined approach not only ensures efficient shipping but also aligns the process with sustainable practices focused on reducing environmental impact.
