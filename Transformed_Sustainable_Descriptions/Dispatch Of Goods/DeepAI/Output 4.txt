If goods shall be shipped, the secretary initiates a digital communication platform to clarify who will coordinate the shipping. For larger quantities, a sustainable shipping solution will be sourced. In these cases, the secretary invites three eco-friendly logistics companies that utilize electric or low-emission vehicles to submit their offers through a digital platform and selects one based on criteria that prioritize low carbon emissions and energy efficiency. For smaller quantities, a streamlined process is implemented where normal post shipment is utilized, with the package label generated digitally and shared via email, eliminating paper waste. The logistics department head reviews parcel insurance options digitally, ensuring it aligns with sustainable practices and is only purchased when absolutely necessary. Meanwhile, the warehousemen prepare the goods for packaging using recycled materials and sustainable practices, optimizing packaging design to minimize waste. Packaging materials are sourced from renewable resources or are recyclable to further reduce environmental impact. Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for pick-up by the chosen logistics company, and real-time tracking information is shared digitally with relevant stakeholders to enhance transparency while reducing logistical emissions. All documentation pertaining to the shipment is managed electronically to avoid paper waste, ensuring a comprehensive approach to sustainability and efficiency throughout the entire process.
