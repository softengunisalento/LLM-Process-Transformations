If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping through a digital platform, avoiding physical paperwork. For shipments of large amounts, special eco-friendly shipping will be necessary, such as using electric or low-emission vehicles. In these cases, the secretary requests offers from three logistics companies that use low-carbon or renewable energy sources through an online bidding system. The secretary selects one of them based on the lowest environmental impact and best service. In the case of small amounts, a green postal service, using electric delivery options or bikes, will be selected. The secretary digitally generates the package label using green software that optimizes energy consumption, and an automatic email notification is sent to the logistics department head to take out parcel insurance online if necessary, depending on the environmental risks involved.

Meanwhile, the goods can already be packaged by the warehousemen using recycled or biodegradable materials, reducing packaging waste. The packaging process is designed to minimize production waste by optimizing the material use and energy consumption, powered by renewable energy sources like solar panels.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the selected logistic company. The pick-up process is optimized to minimize idle time and emissions, with real-time tracking and scheduling through a cloud-based green IT system. All communications and documentation are handled digitally to reduce paper waste and energy consumption associated with printing and physical delivery.
