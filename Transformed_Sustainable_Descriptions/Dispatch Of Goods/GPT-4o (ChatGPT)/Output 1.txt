If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping through a digital logistics management system to minimize paper usage. If you have large amounts, special shipping will be necessary. In these cases, the secretary invites three logistic companies via an automated email or an online platform that filters for companies using electric or low-emission vehicles to reduce carbon footprint. The offers are compared in the system, considering eco-friendly criteria such as the use of renewable energy and carbon offsetting. The logistics company with the most sustainable shipping practices is selected through the system.

In case of small amounts, a green-certified courier service with electric or hybrid vehicles is used. A digital shipping label is generated by the system, and a parcel insurance request is automatically forwarded to the logistics department head if necessary. The entire process is managed electronically to reduce energy consumption and waste related to physical paperwork.

In the meantime, the goods can be already packaged by the warehouse staff using eco-friendly, recyclable packaging materials. The packaging process should aim to minimize waste, and any waste produced should be properly sorted for recycling. Energy-efficient lighting and machinery powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind, should be used in the warehouse to reduce energy consumption during the packaging process.

If everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the logistic company using vehicles powered by electricity or other low-emission technologies. The system sends an automated notification to the logistic company for pick-up scheduling, avoiding the need for printed documents or manual communication, further reducing the carbon footprint of the process.
