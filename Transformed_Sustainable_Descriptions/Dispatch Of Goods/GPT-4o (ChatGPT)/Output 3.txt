If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping. Priority is given to logistic companies using electric or low-emission vehicles. If you have large amounts, special shipping will be necessary, with preference given to companies offering carbon-neutral shipping solutions. In these cases, the secretary sends digital invitations to three logistic companies to make offers, using green software platforms that optimize energy efficiency, and she selects the one with the lowest environmental impact.

For small amounts, eco-friendly postal services are used, preferably those with carbon offset programs. The package label is generated digitally, avoiding the use of paper, and the logistics department head assesses whether parcel insurance is necessary through a digital system that avoids printing documents.

In the meantime, the goods can already be packaged by the warehousemen using sustainable, recycled, or biodegradable materials, and they follow a low-energy process. Energy used in packaging is minimized by optimizing the workflow and relying on renewable energy sources for the warehouse operations.

If everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the chosen logistics company, which operates using electric or hybrid vehicles. Digital tracking of the package ensures no need for physical paperwork, reducing waste further and enhancing efficiency.
