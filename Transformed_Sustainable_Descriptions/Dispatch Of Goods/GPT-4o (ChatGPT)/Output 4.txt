If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping. Preference is given to logistic companies that use electric or low-emission vehicles to reduce the carbon footprint. If you have large amounts, special shipping will be necessary, but priority is given to providers with sustainable shipping methods. In these cases, the secretary invites three eco-certified logistic companies to submit digital offers through an online platform, and one of them is selected based on both cost-effectiveness and environmental impact.

In case of small amounts, normal post shipment is used, preferably with low-emission or electric vehicle options. The secretary generates a digital package label, which is electronically forwarded to the logistics department. If parcel insurance is necessary, it is requested via a digital platform by the logistics department head, avoiding paper documentation.

In the meantime, the goods can already be packaged by the warehousemen using recyclable or biodegradable packaging materials, minimizing waste. The warehouse uses energy-efficient practices, such as LED lighting and equipment powered by renewable energy sources like solar panels.

Once everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the logistic company. The pick-up is scheduled digitally to optimize vehicle routes, reducing fuel consumption.
