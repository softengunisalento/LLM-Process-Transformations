If goods shall be shipped, the secretary checks an online platform to determine which logistics provider offers the most eco-friendly shipping options. The platform prioritizes electric or low-emission vehicles for deliveries. If large amounts of goods are to be shipped, special eco-friendly shipping will be necessary. In these cases, the secretary sends digital requests for offers to three logistics companies with a preference for low-carbon solutions. The offers are reviewed digitally, and the one with the lowest environmental impact is selected. For small amounts, carbon-neutral post shipment is chosen. The secretary generates an electronic package label, and if needed, the logistics department head arranges parcel insurance digitally through an eco-friendly insurer.

While this process is ongoing, the warehouse staff, using energy-efficient machinery powered by renewable energy sources, can already begin packaging the goods. Recycled or biodegradable packaging materials are used to minimize waste. Any packaging waste is collected for recycling.

Once everything is prepared, the goods are placed in the designated area for pick-up, ensuring that packaging and storage are handled efficiently to reduce waste. When the logistics company arrives, the goods are handed over, ensuring that only electric or low-emission vehicles are used for transportation.
