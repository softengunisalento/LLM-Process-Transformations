If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping using an online platform. If you have large amounts, special shipping will be necessary. In these cases, the secretary invites three logistic companies to make offers via email and selects one of them based on their use of electric or low-emission vehicles. In case of small amounts, normal post shipment is used with preference for low-emission options. Therefore, a digital package label is created by the secretary and a parcel insurance is taken by the logistics department head if necessary, using an online system.

In the meantime, the goods can already be packaged by the warehousemen using recycled and recyclable materials. If everything is ready, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the logistic company, ensuring that the vehicles used are electric or low-emission. The entire process is monitored using green software solutions to minimize energy use and optimize efficiency. Renewable energy sources are used to power the facilities, and any waste materials are recycled appropriately to avoid production waste.
