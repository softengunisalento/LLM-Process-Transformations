A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry. The restaurant is equipped with renewable energy sources, such as solar panels and energy-efficient lighting. The guest uses a digital menu, accessible via a QR code on the table, to browse the meal options. This system is connected to a cloud service running on green software that optimizes resource allocation to minimize energy consumption. The menu displays meals made from locally sourced ingredients, minimizing transportation emissions, and features options that use seasonal produce to reduce energy required for storage.

The guest selects a dish from the menu. Instead of waiting in line, the order is placed directly through the digital system. The POS system is cloud-based and runs on energy-efficient servers powered by renewable energy. The system notifies the kitchen of the order in real time without any need for physical interaction. The guest pays via a digital wallet, reducing the need for printed receipts. The transaction confirmation is sent via email or an app notification.

The kitchen is optimized for energy efficiency. The chef prepares the meal using low-energy cooking appliances, such as induction stoves and energy-efficient ovens. Throughout the cooking process, waste is minimized by composting organic materials and recycling packaging. Any leftover food is repurposed for future meals or donated to local food banks, avoiding food waste. The kitchen appliances are connected to an energy management system that monitors and adjusts energy use in real time.

Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a reusable container, avoiding single-use plastics. The container is then placed in the service hatch. The digital system automatically notifies the guest through an app that their meal is ready. Instead of using a physical buzzer, the system sends a notification to the guest's phone. The guest is informed that they can now collect their meal.

When the guest arrives at the service hatch, the employee hands over the meal in the reusable container. If the guest does not respond to the app notification within 5 minutes, the system sends an additional reminder. All interactions are digital, eliminating the need for physical paper or waste. Any further follow-up or issues can be managed through the same app, maintaining a seamless and sustainable experience throughout the process.

Throughout this entire process, the restaurant operates on a zero-waste policy, recycles all possible materials, and ensures minimal energy use through advanced energy management systems. The software and hardware infrastructure is designed for minimal environmental impact, leveraging green software principles.
