A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry. Upon entry, he is directed to an interactive digital menu displayed on an energy-efficient tablet or his own device via a QR code to browse the changing meal range. The digital menu highlights eco-friendly, locally sourced meals to encourage sustainable choices. After making his selection, the guest places his order through the digital interface, avoiding the need for employee intervention at this stage. The order is then transmitted directly to the POS system, which operates on green software designed for optimal energy efficiency, and the guest is charged via a contactless payment method using his smartphone, reducing the need for physical cash handling.

Once the payment is confirmed, a digital notification is sent to the guest's mobile device, replacing the need for a physical buzzer. The notification informs the guest that he will receive a message when his meal is ready. The employee is notified digitally of the new order and passes it on to the kitchen electronically. The kitchen operates on energy-efficient appliances powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind power, to prepare the meal.

The chef prepares the meal using a low-waste approach, ensuring minimal food waste by optimizing ingredient usage. Organic waste is collected separately for composting, and non-recyclable waste is minimized through careful inventory management. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in the service hatch and sends an electronic notification to the employee. The employee receives this notification and, using the green software, triggers the guest's mobile notification instead of setting off a physical buzzer.

The guest is informed digitally via his device that his meal is ready for collection. When the guest approaches the service hatch, the employee hands over the meal in sustainable, recyclable, or reusable packaging to further reduce environmental impact. If the guest does not respond to the digital notification within 5 minutes, an additional message is automatically sent to remind him that his meal is ready, avoiding unnecessary calls or announcements.

Throughout this process, the restaurant utilizes energy-efficient lighting and climate control systems powered by renewable energy sources to maintain a comfortable dining environment with minimal carbon footprint. The restaurant also encourages guests to properly dispose of or recycle their waste by providing clearly marked bins for sorting recyclables, organic waste, and non-recyclable materials, helping to minimize the environmental impact of the restaurant's operations.
