A guest enters the restaurant after receiving a notification on their phone, indicating that it's a good time to visit based on their preferences and the availability of sustainable meals. The guest reviews the digital menu on their phone, which is updated in real-time to reflect seasonal and locally sourced ingredients that minimize food waste. After making a selection, the guest places their order digitally via the restaurant’s mobile app, reducing the need for physical interaction. 

The order is automatically entered into the restaurant’s green software system, which is optimized for energy efficiency and low-resource consumption. The system processes the payment online, eliminating the need for physical cash transactions. Once the order is confirmed, the system notifies the guest through the app with instructions to wait until their meal is prepared. Meanwhile, the system sends the meal order directly to the chef using a secure, energy-efficient digital communication system.

The kitchen operates on renewable energy sources like solar or wind, reducing the carbon footprint associated with food preparation. The chef prepares the meal using ingredients that are measured and portioned to avoid excess waste. Any organic waste generated during food preparation is composted or sent to a local recycling facility. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a reusable, eco-friendly container at the service hatch. The system automatically informs both the employee and the guest through the app that the meal is ready for pickup.

The guest receives a notification on their phone, eliminating the need for physical buzzers and reducing the use of non-recyclable materials. Upon arriving at the service hatch, the guest collects their meal, which is handed over by the employee using contactless service methods to further reduce unnecessary energy consumption. If a guest does not collect the meal within a set time, the system automatically sends a reminder through the app, avoiding the need for loud verbal calls, which can increase stress and energy consumption in the environment.

Throughout the entire process, the restaurant employs low-energy lighting and appliances, and the digital infrastructure runs on green software, ensuring minimal energy use during order processing and meal preparation. Any packaging or materials used during the meal preparation are either reusable or recyclable, ensuring a closed-loop system that significantly reduces waste and promotes sustainability.
