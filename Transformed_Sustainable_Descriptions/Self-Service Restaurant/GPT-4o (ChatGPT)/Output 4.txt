A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry. Upon entry, the guest is informed through a digital screen about available meal options. The menu is adjusted in real-time based on seasonal, locally sourced ingredients to minimize transportation-related carbon emissions. The guest selects their meal via a tablet, reducing paper use and avoiding the energy consumption tied to printing menus. Once the selection is made, the order is sent digitally to the kitchen.

The employee receives the order on a digital POS system, which is powered by renewable energy sources such as solar panels. Instead of a buzzer, the guest receives a digital notification via a mobile application or SMS when their order is ready, reducing the production of plastic buzzers and electronic waste. The employee confirms the order payment, which is completed via contactless payment to eliminate physical cash handling and reduce time spent per transaction. All receipts are emailed to the guest to reduce paper waste.

The kitchen operates with energy-efficient appliances and makes use of food scraps for composting or recycling purposes to minimize food waste. The chef prepares the meal using ingredients sourced from sustainable suppliers, reducing the environmental impact of transport and packaging. Waste materials from food preparation are sorted and recycled, where possible, and organic waste is composted.

Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a reusable container at the service hatch and digitally notifies the employee through a green software system. The system is optimized to minimize server power usage through efficient coding practices. Upon receiving the notification, the employee activates a digital signal via the mobile app or SMS to inform the guest that their meal is ready for collection. The guest is encouraged to bring their own reusable packaging or is provided with biodegradable containers if needed. As soon as the guest arrives at the service hatch, the employee hands over the meal in eco-friendly packaging.

Should the guest not respond to the notification, a reminder is sent automatically through the digital system every five minutes, with a limit to avoid excessive energy consumption. No manual calling is required, reducing unnecessary labor and energy expenditure. After the guest consumes the meal, they are encouraged to recycle any remaining packaging through designated recycling stations within the restaurant, contributing to the overall sustainability of the process.
