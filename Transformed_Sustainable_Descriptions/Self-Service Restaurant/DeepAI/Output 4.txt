A guest enters the restaurant feeling hungry and is greeted by a digital menu displayed on eco-friendly screens that feature locally sourced seasonal dishes to reduce carbon emissions associated with transport. The guest selects a dish and can view the carbon footprint associated with each meal option. Once the guest has made their choice, they are prompted to place their order via a mobile app or a self-service kiosk that minimizes paper use. The order is confirmed digitally, and payment is processed through secure apps that support mobile payments, eliminating the need for physical cash handling.

Upon payment, the guest receives a notification on their smartphone indicating that their meal is being prepared, which eliminates the need for a physical buzzer. The restaurant staff is notified of the new meal order electronically, thus reducing paper waste. The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient appliances powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind power. The food is prepared in batches to optimize energy use, and any kitchen waste is composted or recycled appropriately to avoid landfill contributions.

Once the meal is ready, the chef updates the digital system, which notifies both the guest's smartphone and the employee. The employee can now track the preparation and pickup times digitally. Should the guest not respond to the notification within a specified time frame, an automated alert is sent personally via the app, reducing the need for vocal calls in the restaurant space, which also contributes to a quieter dining environment.

When the guest arrives at the service hatch, they simply present their mobile device, ensuring a contactless pickup. The employee hands over the meal, and any recyclable packaging is included for the guest to dispose of responsibly. The restaurant operates with low-emission vehicles for deliveries and waste collection, further reducing the overall carbon footprint. The entire process is designed to support green software principles, optimizing for low energy consumption and minimal environmental impact throughout the dining experience.
