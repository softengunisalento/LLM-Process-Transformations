A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry, ideally having checked the menu online to reduce paper waste. He chooses a dish from the seasonal, locally-sourced meal range designed to have minimal environmental impact and waits in a designated area that promotes social distancing. Following this, he places his order with the employee using a digital ordering platform accessible via his smartphone, reducing the need for physical menus and minimizing food waste by allowing real-time updates on availability. The employee enters the order into a sustainable Point of Sale (POS) system that runs on renewable energy sources and prompts the guest for electronic payment, encouraging the use of contactless transactions to limit cash handling. After the payment, the employee instead of using a buzzer, sends a notification to the guest’s phone with the message: "Your dinner is ready for collection when you receive this alert." 

Subsequently, the employee informs the chef of the new meal order via a sustainable digital system integrated directly with kitchen operations, streamlining communication. The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient cooking methods and places it in an insulated service hatch designed to maintain optimal temperature without high energy use. He then sends a notification via the same system to confirm that the finished meal is placed in the service hatch. The employee, upon receiving the alert that the meal is ready, informs the guest through the app notification rather than sounding a buzzer, promoting a quieter dining atmosphere.

As the guest receives the notification, he approaches the service hatch to collect his meal. The employee hands over the meal while ensuring it is packaged in eco-friendly, biodegradable materials designed to minimize waste. Should the guest not appear to collect the meal within a specified timeframe, the employee uses the digital app to send reminders to the guest’s phone rather than making repeated calls, thereby conserving energy used in unnecessary communication.

Any food waste is monitored and collected for composting or animal feed, where applicable, and all recyclable materials from meal preparation and service are separated and processed in an environmentally-friendly manner. The restaurant continuously seeks to improve its sustainability practices, utilizing renewable energy sources and focusing on green software solutions that minimize digital energy usage and enhance overall operational efficiency.
