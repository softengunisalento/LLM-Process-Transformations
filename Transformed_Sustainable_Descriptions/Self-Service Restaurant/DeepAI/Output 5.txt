A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry, guided by digital signage that displays real-time menu options sourced from local farms to promote sustainability. He chooses a dish from the changing meal range, which is prepared with seasonal ingredients to minimize transport emissions, and waits until it is his turn via a virtual queuing system on his smartphone, reducing congestion in the waiting area. Following this, he places his order through a mobile app that communicates directly with the point-of-sale (POS) system, thus limiting physical contact and paper use. The app processes the payment digitally, allowing for a cashless transaction, which also enhances efficiency and reduces the need for cash handling.

After the payment, the app provides an electronic notification to the guest with the message: "Your dinner is being prepared. You will be notified when it is ready." The employee immediately receives an alert on their device about the new meal order, and the kitchen staff, equipped with energy-efficient appliances powered by renewable energy sources, prepares the meal using the freshest available ingredients.

Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a designated service area designed to keep meals at optimal temperatures without additional heating requirements. The chef then sends a confirmation via the system to notify the employee that the meal is prepared. As soon as the employee is made aware that the meal is ready, they trigger the guest's electronic notification. This reduces the need for physical buzzers and supports a contactless dining experience.

The guest, upon receiving the notification, walks to the service area where the employee, equipped with digital tools, verifies the order using the app. The employee confirms the order by scanning a QR code presented by the guest on their smartphone. The meal is handed over in eco-friendly, compostable packaging, and the unnecessary waste is kept to a minimum. Should a guest not respond to the notification, the app will send a follow-up digital reminder after 5 minutes, ensuring that the communication remains efficient and paperless. If necessary, the employee can use the app to send a direct message to the guest for a more effective reach. 

Additionally, the restaurant implements a waste management system that separates food scraps for composting, while any non-compostable materials are recycled properly. The restaurant commits to regular audits of its energy consumption, continuously seeking ways to improve operational efficiency and reduce its carbon footprint through technology and sustainable practices.
