Here's a more sustainable and digitalized version of the BPMN process description:

A guest enters the eco-friendly restaurant, which uses renewable energy sources and energy-efficient appliances, when feeling hungry. The guest accesses the digital menu on their smartphone or a provided tablet, which displays the changing meal range with carbon footprint information for each dish. The guest virtually joins a queue through the restaurant's app, eliminating the need for physical waiting. When it's their turn, they place their order through the app, which automatically notifies the employee. The employee confirms the order on their energy-efficient POS system, which is designed with green software principles to minimize energy consumption. The guest completes the payment electronically through the app or contactless payment methods, reducing paper waste from receipts. After payment, the app automatically generates a digital notification system for the guest, informing them: "You will receive a notification when your meal is ready."

The POS system, running on green cloud infrastructure, automatically informs the chef of the new meal order through a low-energy display in the kitchen. The chef, using energy-efficient cooking equipment and locally sourced, seasonal ingredients to reduce transportation emissions, prepares the meal. The chef uses precise portioning techniques to minimize food waste. Any unavoidable food scraps are collected for composting. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in an energy-efficient service hatch and marks it as complete on the kitchen management system.

The employee receives an automatic notification on their device when the meal is ready. This triggers a notification sent directly to the guest's smartphone or the provided tablet. The guest is informed that their meal is ready for collection through this digital alert system, eliminating the need for battery-powered buzzers. The guest can then approach the service hatch to collect their meal, which is served in compostable or reusable containers. If a guest doesn't respond to the digital notification within 5 minutes, the system automatically sends a follow-up reminder. If necessary, it continues to send reminders at regular intervals.

After the meal, the guest is encouraged to use the restaurant's clearly marked recycling and composting stations for any waste. The restaurant also offers a small discount for guests who bring their own reusable containers for takeaway orders. All cleaning products used in the restaurant are eco-friendly and biodegradable. The restaurant's energy usage, water consumption, and waste production are constantly monitored through smart sensors and analytics software to identify areas for further sustainability improvements. Staff members receive regular training on sustainable practices and are encouraged to suggest innovations to reduce the restaurant's environmental impact.
