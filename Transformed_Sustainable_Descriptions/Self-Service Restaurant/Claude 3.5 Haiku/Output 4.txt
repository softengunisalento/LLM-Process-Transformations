Here's a more sustainable and digitalized version of the BPMN process description:

A guest enters the eco-friendly restaurant, which uses energy-efficient LED lighting and is powered by renewable energy sources, when feeling hungry. The guest accesses the digital menu on their smartphone or a provided tablet, which showcases a changing range of locally-sourced, seasonal dishes with their carbon footprint displayed. The guest virtually joins a digital queue system, eliminating the need for physical waiting. When it's their turn, they place their order through the restaurant's mobile app or on the provided tablet. The app automatically enters the order into the energy-efficient POS system, which runs on green cloud infrastructure. The guest pays electronically, reducing the need for cash handling. After payment, the system sends a digital notification to the guest's device, informing them that they will receive an alert when their meal is ready.

The system automatically notifies the chef of the new meal order through an energy-efficient kitchen display system. The chef, using energy-efficient appliances and cooking methods that minimize heat waste, prepares the meal using locally-sourced ingredients to reduce transportation emissions. The chef uses precise portioning to avoid food waste and composts any unavoidable organic waste. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in an insulated service hatch to maintain temperature without additional energy use. The kitchen display system automatically updates to show the meal is ready.

The restaurant management system, running on energy-efficient servers, detects that the meal is ready and sends a digital notification to the guest's device. This notification informs the guest that their meal is ready for collection. The guest can then approach the service hatch to collect their meal. They are encouraged to eat in-house using reusable tableware, but if taking away, they are provided with compostable or recyclable containers. As the guest collects their meal, the employee confirms the handover in the system. If a guest doesn't respond to the digital notification within 5 minutes, the system automatically sends follow-up notifications at regular intervals.

Throughout this process, the restaurant uses a smart energy management system to optimize heating, cooling, and ventilation based on occupancy and weather conditions. Water-saving fixtures are installed in restrooms, and all cleaning products used are eco-friendly. The restaurant also implements a comprehensive recycling and composting program for any waste generated during the dining experience. Additionally, the restaurant's digital systems are designed following green software principles, optimizing code efficiency to reduce energy consumption and using servers powered by renewable energy.
