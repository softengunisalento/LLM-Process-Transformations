Here's a more sustainable and digitalized version of the BPMN process description:

A guest enters the eco-friendly restaurant, which uses energy-efficient LED lighting and is powered by renewable energy sources, when feeling hungry. The restaurant's menu is displayed on energy-efficient digital screens, reducing paper waste. The guest browses the changing meal range, which emphasizes locally-sourced, seasonal ingredients to minimize transportation emissions. While waiting, the guest can use a mobile app to view real-time queue information, reducing unnecessary standing time and energy consumption. When it's their turn, they place an order through a self-service kiosk or their smartphone, minimizing face-to-face interactions and reducing the need for physical menus. The cloud-based POS system, optimized for energy efficiency, processes the order and facilitates a contactless payment transaction. After payment, the system automatically sends a digital notification to the guest's smartphone, eliminating the need for physical buzzers and reducing electronic waste.

The POS system instantly relays the order to the kitchen's energy-efficient displays. The chef, using energy-star rated appliances and induction cooktops, prepares the meal with a focus on minimizing food waste. Cooking processes are optimized to reduce energy consumption, and any food scraps are composted on-site. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in an insulated, reusable container in the service hatch and updates the order status in the kitchen management system.

The system automatically notifies the guest via their smartphone that the meal is ready for collection. The guest can then pick up their meal from a designated area, reducing queuing and optimizing space usage. If a guest doesn't respond to the digital notification within 5 minutes, the system automatically sends follow-up reminders at intervals, eliminating the need for staff to manually call out.

Throughout the process, the restaurant uses green software practices, including energy-efficient algorithms, cloud computing powered by renewable energy, and software that automatically adjusts system performance based on demand to conserve energy. All digital receipts and transaction records are stored in energy-efficient data centers, reducing paper usage and physical storage needs. The restaurant also employs a smart energy management system that optimizes heating, cooling, and ventilation based on occupancy and time of day, further reducing its carbon footprint.
