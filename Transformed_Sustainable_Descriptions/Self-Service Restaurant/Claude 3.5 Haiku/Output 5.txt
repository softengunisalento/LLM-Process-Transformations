Here's a more sustainable and digitalized version of the BPMN process description:

A guest enters the eco-friendly restaurant, which uses renewable energy sources and energy-efficient appliances, when feeling hungry. The guest views the digital menu on a low-power e-ink display or their own smartphone, showcasing locally-sourced, seasonal dishes that minimize food miles and carbon footprint. The guest waits in a virtual queue managed by a green-coded app, which optimizes wait times and reduces energy consumption. When it's their turn, they place their order using a self-service kiosk powered by solar energy, reducing the need for additional staff and paper waste. The kiosk, running on energy-efficient software, processes the order and accepts payment through contactless methods, eliminating the need for physical cash handling. After payment, the kiosk generates a unique QR code for the guest to scan with their smartphone, which will notify them when their meal is ready.

The order is automatically sent to the kitchen's energy-efficient management system, which optimizes cooking schedules to reduce energy usage. The chef, using energy-star rated appliances and induction cooktops, prepares the meal with minimal food waste, composting any organic scraps. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a smart, insulated service hatch that maintains optimal temperature without excessive energy use. The system automatically updates the order status.

When the meal is ready for collection, the guest receives a push notification on their smartphone. If the guest doesn't collect their meal within 5 minutes, the app sends a gentle reminder notification, conserving energy that would have been used in manual calls or buzzer systems. The guest collects their meal from the service hatch, which opens automatically upon scanning their QR code, minimizing physical contact and energy waste from repeated opening and closing. The guest can then enjoy their meal at tables made from recycled materials, with clearly marked recycling and composting bins nearby for any leftovers or packaging.

Throughout this process, the restaurant uses a cloud-based, energy-efficient software system that optimizes operations, reduces food waste through predictive analytics, and minimizes unnecessary energy consumption. All digital systems in the restaurant are designed with green software principles, using efficient algorithms and data storage practices to reduce their carbon footprint. The restaurant also encourages guests to bring their own reusable containers for takeaway orders, offering a small discount as an incentive to reduce packaging waste.
