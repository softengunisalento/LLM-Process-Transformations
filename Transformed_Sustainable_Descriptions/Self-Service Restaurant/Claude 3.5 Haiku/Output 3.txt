Here's a more sustainable and digitalized version of the BPMN process description:

A guest enters the eco-friendly restaurant, which uses renewable energy sources for lighting and appliances, when feeling hungry. The guest accesses the digital menu on their smartphone or a provided tablet, which displays the changing meal range with carbon footprint information for each dish. The guest waits in a virtual queue managed by an energy-efficient app. When it's their turn, they place their order through the app, which transmits it directly to the employee's low-power consumption device. The employee confirms the order on their device, which automatically updates the restaurant's cloud-based POS system, minimizing paper waste. The guest pays electronically through the app, reducing the need for physical cash handling. After payment, the app sends a digital notification to the guest's device, informing them that they will receive an alert when their meal is ready.

The employee's device automatically notifies the chef of the new meal order through a low-energy consumption kitchen management system. The chef, using energy-efficient cooking equipment and locally sourced, seasonal ingredients to reduce transportation emissions, prepares the meal. The chef utilizes precise portioning techniques to minimize food waste. Any unavoidable food scraps are composted on-site. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in an insulated, reusable container in the service hatch and marks it as complete in the kitchen management system.

The system immediately sends a notification to the guest's device, informing them that their meal is ready for collection. If the guest doesn't respond within 5 minutes, the system automatically sends a gentle reminder. The employee, alerted by their device, retrieves the meal from the service hatch when the guest approaches. The guest can then enjoy their meal in the restaurant's dining area, which is equipped with energy-efficient climate control systems and LED lighting. After finishing, the guest is encouraged to sort any remaining waste into clearly marked recycling and composting bins. The reusable container is collected, sanitized using water-efficient, eco-friendly cleaning processes, and returned to circulation.

Throughout this process, the restaurant's green software systems optimize energy use, adjusting lighting and climate control based on occupancy and time of day. The digital ordering and queue management systems reduce paper waste and improve operational efficiency. All electronic devices used in the process are energy-efficient models, and the restaurant's IT infrastructure is designed to minimize energy consumption, utilizing cloud services powered by renewable energy where possible. The restaurant also regularly analyzes data from these systems to further refine their processes and reduce their overall environmental impact.
