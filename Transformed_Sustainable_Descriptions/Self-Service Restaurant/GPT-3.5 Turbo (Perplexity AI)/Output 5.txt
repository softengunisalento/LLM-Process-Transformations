A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry, greeted by digital signage displaying the menu, which emphasizes seasonal and locally sourced dishes. The guest selects a dish from the changing meal range, which is designed to minimize food waste by offering smaller portions and encouraging sharing. The guest then waits in a designated area equipped with comfortable seating and greenery to enhance the atmosphere while reducing energy use through natural ventilation.

Once it is the guest's turn, they place their order using a digital kiosk or mobile app, which streamlines the process and reduces paper usage. The system automatically calculates the total and allows for contactless payment options, minimizing cash handling. After payment, the system generates a digital notification for the guest, indicating that their meal will be ready shortly.

The employee receives the order digitally and inputs it directly into an energy-efficient POS system that is optimized for low power consumption. The employee informs the chef of the new meal order through a digital communication platform, reducing the need for physical interaction and paper notes.

The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient kitchen appliances powered by renewable energy sources. Ingredients are sourced from local suppliers to further reduce carbon emissions associated with transportation. Any food scraps generated during preparation are composted or recycled to minimize waste.

Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in a designated service hatch equipped with an energy-efficient heating lamp that uses minimal energy to keep meals warm without excessive power consumption. The chef then updates the system to notify the employee that the meal is ready.

The employee receives an automatic alert on their device when the meal is ready. Instead of using a buzzer, guests receive a push notification on their mobile device, reducing electronic waste and improving efficiency. If a guest does not respond within 5 minutes, the system sends a gentle reminder notification rather than requiring multiple calls from staff.

When the guest arrives at the service hatch, they present their digital order confirmation on their device. The employee hands over the meal while ensuring proper hygiene protocols are followed. Any packaging used is compostable or made from recycled materials to support sustainability initiatives.

Overall, this process emphasizes digitalization, renewable energy use, waste reduction, and efficient resource management to create a more sustainable dining experience while maintaining high service quality.
