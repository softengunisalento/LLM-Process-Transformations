A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry, greeted by a digital menu displayed on a tablet or screen. The menu showcases a selection of dishes made from locally sourced, seasonal ingredients, emphasizing sustainability. The guest can browse the changing meal range and place their order directly through the digital interface, reducing paper waste and energy consumption associated with printed menus.

Once the order is placed, the system notifies the employee via a mobile device, eliminating the need for physical order slips. The employee prepares to collect payment using a contactless payment system, which reduces cash handling and minimizes energy use associated with traditional payment methods. After payment is completed, the employee activates a digital notification system that sends a message to the guest's smartphone or device, stating: "Your meal is being prepared. You will be notified when it is ready."

The employee then informs the chef of the new meal order via an internal messaging system, streamlining communication and reducing paper usage. The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient kitchen appliances powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind power. Once the meal is ready, the chef places it in an insulated, reusable container designed to maintain temperature while minimizing waste.

The chef then sends a digital notification to the employee indicating that the meal is ready for pickup. Upon receiving this notification, the employee activates the guest's digital alert system to inform them that their meal is ready for collection. If the guest does not respond within five minutes, the system automatically sends a gentle reminder notification to their device.

When the guest arrives at the service hatch, they can scan a QR code on their smartphone to confirm their identity and collect their meal without physical contact. The employee hands over the meal in its reusable container while reminding the guest about proper disposal and recycling practices for any packaging materials.

After each service, any food waste is collected in designated compost bins for recycling, while other waste materials are sorted into recycling streams. The restaurant continuously monitors its energy usage and waste production through a digital dashboard that tracks sustainability metrics, allowing for ongoing improvements in operational efficiency and environmental impact. This process not only enhances guest experience but also aligns with sustainable practices aimed at reducing carbon footprint and promoting environmental responsibility.
