A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry. He chooses a dish from the changing meal range, which features locally sourced and seasonal ingredients to minimize carbon emissions. Instead of waiting in line, the guest uses a mobile app to place his order digitally, reducing energy consumption associated with in-person interactions. After placing the order, the app provides an estimated wait time and a notification system to alert the guest when it is their turn.

The employee receives the digital order on a tablet linked to the POS system, which is powered by renewable energy sources. The employee confirms the order and processes payment through the app, eliminating the need for cash transactions and reducing paper waste. After payment, the app sends a notification to the guest stating: "Your meal will be ready shortly."

The employee informs the chef of the new meal order via a digital communication platform, ensuring quick and efficient communication without paper usage. The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient kitchen appliances and practices zero-waste cooking techniques, utilizing all parts of the ingredients where possible. Once prepared, the chef places the finished meal in a designated service area and updates the system to indicate that it is ready for collection.

The employee receives a notification on their device when the meal is ready and activates a digital buzzer system that alerts the guest through their mobile app. This reduces unnecessary noise pollution and energy use associated with physical buzzers. If the guest does not respond within 5 minutes, a gentle reminder is sent via push notification instead of calling out.

When the guest arrives at the service area, they can scan a QR code displayed on their mobile device to confirm their identity and collect their meal from a designated pickup station. The employee ensures that any waste generated during meal preparation is sorted for recycling or composting, adhering to sustainable waste management practices. The process emphasizes minimal production waste by utilizing ingredients efficiently and recycling packaging materials where applicable.
