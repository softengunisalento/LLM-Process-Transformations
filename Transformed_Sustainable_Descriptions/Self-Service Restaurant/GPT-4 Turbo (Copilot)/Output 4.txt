A guest enters the restaurant when feeling hungry. He chooses a dish from the changing meal range displayed on a digital menu board and waits until it is his turn. Following this, he places his order through a digital kiosk or mobile app. The order is automatically entered into the POS system, and the guest makes a digital payment using a contactless method. After the payment, the system sends a notification to the guest's mobile device with the following information: "You will receive a notification when your dinner is ready."

The system automatically informs the chef of the new meal order. The chef prepares the meal using energy-efficient kitchen appliances and places it in the service hatch. He then updates the system to indicate that the meal is ready.

As soon as the system is updated, it sends a notification to the guest's mobile device. This is how the guest finds out that his meal is ready for collection. He can pick up his meal and eat it. As soon as the guest appears at the service hatch, the employee hands over his meal. Should a guest not react to the notification, the system sends a reminder after 5 minutes, if necessary several times in a row.

The restaurant uses electric or low-emission vehicles for any necessary shipping and delivery. High energy use is reduced by utilizing energy-efficient appliances and lighting. Waste materials are recycled, and renewable energy sources are used to power the restaurant. Production waste is minimized through careful inventory management and portion control. The entire process is digitalized to reduce paper waste and improve efficiency, focusing on green software solutions to manage orders and notifications.
