If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I gather information digitally through secure online databases to minimize paper usage. I check that case using energy-efficient software tools, ensuring that my actions are optimized for low energy consumption. If the possibility is confirmed, I send a request for payment to the insurant via email, eliminating the need for physical delivery and reducing carbon emissions. I set a digital reminder in my calendar to follow up on this request.

If recourse is not possible, I close the case electronically, ensuring that all records are stored in a cloud-based system powered by renewable energy sources. Upon receiving the payment, I process the booking using automated accounting software that operates on green technology, and then I close the case digitally.

In cases where the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning using a digital platform designed for efficient document management. If their claim is valid, I close the case electronically. If their reasoning is found to be incorrect, I prepare a digital file and forward it to a collection agency that practices sustainable operations, such as using electric vehicles for any necessary transportation.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and we have not received any payment, I again forward the case to the collection agency electronically. Throughout this process, I ensure that all waste materials are recycled appropriately and that we minimize production waste by relying on digital documentation rather than physical copies. By focusing on green software solutions and digital communication, I contribute to a more sustainable workflow while maintaining efficiency in handling subrogation cases.
