If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I gather information digitally through a secure online platform to minimize paper usage and reduce carbon emissions. I then conduct a virtual review of the case using energy-efficient software tools that operate on renewable energy sources. If the possibility for recourse is confirmed, I send a digital request for payment via email to the insurant, eliminating the need for physical mail and ensuring a quick response time. I also set a digital reminder in my calendar system to follow up on this request.

In cases where recourse is not possible, I close the case digitally by updating our electronic records, ensuring no physical documents are created. Upon receiving payment, I record the transaction electronically and finalize the case in our digital management system, again avoiding any paper waste. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning through a secure online communication channel. If their claim is valid, I simply update the case status in our digital system and close it.

If their disagreement is unfounded, I prepare a digital file with all relevant documentation and forward it to a collection agency via secure electronic transfer, reducing transportation emissions associated with physical document delivery. If the deadline for disagreement is reached and we have not received any payment, I also forward the case electronically to the collection agency, ensuring all processes are completed without unnecessary waste or high energy use. Throughout this process, I ensure that all software used is optimized for energy efficiency and adheres to green software principles, thus contributing to a lower overall carbon footprint.
