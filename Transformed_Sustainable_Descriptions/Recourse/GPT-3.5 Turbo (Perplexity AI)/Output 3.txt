If an insurant could be possibly subrogated against, I gather relevant information through digital platforms to minimize paper usage and energy consumption. I check the case using a cloud-based system that operates on renewable energy sources. If the possibility of subrogation is confirmed, I send a digital request for payment to the insurant via email, which eliminates the need for physical mail and reduces carbon emissions from transportation. I also set a digital reminder in my task management software to track follow-ups, ensuring efficient resource use.

If recourse is not possible, I close the case electronically, ensuring all documentation is stored in a digital format to minimize waste. Upon receiving the payment, I record the transaction in our financial software that is optimized for energy efficiency and powered by renewable energy, then close the case digitally.

If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning through our electronic case management system. If their reasoning is valid, I close the case digitally. If it is not valid, I prepare a digital file to forward the case to a collection agency using secure electronic transmission to avoid physical document handling and reduce waste.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and we have not received any payment, I also forward the case to the collection agency electronically. Throughout this process, I ensure that all electronic devices are energy-efficient and that we utilize green software solutions to further reduce our carbon footprint and promote sustainability in our operations.
