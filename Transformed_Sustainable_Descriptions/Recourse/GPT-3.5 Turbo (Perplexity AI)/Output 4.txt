If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I gather information digitally using a secure online platform, minimizing paper use. I check the case remotely, and if the possibility is confirmed, I send a digital request for payment to the insurant via email, ensuring that all communications are energy-efficient and utilize green software solutions. I set a digital reminder for follow-up. If recourse is not possible, I close the case electronically, avoiding unnecessary physical documentation.

Upon receiving the payment, I process the transaction through an online accounting system that operates on renewable energy sources and minimizes energy consumption. I then close the case digitally. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning through a secure digital portal. If their claim is valid, I close the case electronically. If their claim is unfounded, I prepare the necessary documentation for forwarding to a collection agency via email to reduce carbon emissions associated with physical mail.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and we have not received any payment, I also forward the case to the collection agency electronically. Throughout this process, I ensure that all waste materials are recycled properly and that any digital tools used are optimized for energy efficiency to further reduce our carbon footprint.
