If an insurant could be possibly subrogated against, I gather information about that through digital channels to minimize paper use. I check that case using a cloud-based system to ensure efficient data processing and reduce energy consumption. If the possibility of subrogation is confirmed, I send a digital request for payment to the insurant via email, eliminating the need for physical mail. I also create a digital reminder in my task management software to track follow-ups. If recourse is not possible, I close the case electronically, ensuring that all documentation is stored in a digital format to reduce waste.

When we receive the payment, I record the transaction in our financial software, which operates on renewable energy sources, and then I close the case digitally. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning using our electronic case management system. If their reasoning is valid, I close the case digitally. If it is not valid, I prepare a digital file to forward the case to a collection agency through secure electronic transmission, minimizing physical document handling.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and we have not received any payment, I also forward the case to the collection agency electronically. Throughout this process, I ensure that all electronic devices are energy-efficient and that we utilize green software solutions to further reduce our carbon footprint.
