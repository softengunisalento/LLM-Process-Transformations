If there is a potential for subrogation against an insurant, I access the necessary information digitally through secure online platforms to reduce paper usage. I conduct a virtual review of the case, and if subrogation is deemed possible, I send a digital request for payment to the insurant via email, utilizing e-signatures to eliminate the need for physical documents. I create a digital reminder for follow-up actions using a task management application powered by renewable energy. If recourse is not feasible, I close the case in the digital system, ensuring a paperless process.

Upon receiving payment, I record the transaction electronically in our financial software that prioritizes energy efficiency, and I finalize the case digitally. In instances where the insurant disputes the recourse, I review their reasoning using an online collaboration tool to minimize energy consumption from unnecessary travel. If the insurant's claim is validated, I close the case digitally. If their reasoning is found to be incorrect, I prepare a digital case file and forward it to an eco-friendly collection agency that employs sustainable practices.

If the deadline for the insurant's disagreement is reached without payment received, I also digitally forward the case to the collection agency, ensuring that all communications and documentation are handled electronically to minimize our environmental impact. Throughout the process, I strive to minimize energy use by utilizing energy-efficient devices and working in an environment that supports renewable energy sources, while also ensuring any physical materials produced or required are recyclable to reduce waste.
