If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I retrieve information about that through a digital database to minimize paper usage and increase efficiency. I analyze the case digitally, using analytical tools that require minimal energy consumption, and if the possibility is confirmed, I send a digital request for payment to the insurant via secure email, which reduces carbon emissions associated with physical mail. Additionally, I set a digital reminder on an energy-efficient platform to follow up. If recourse is indeed not feasible, I digitally archive the case and close it in the system.

Upon receiving the payment, I process the transaction using an online booking system powered by renewable energy sources, then I digitally record the closure of the case. If the insurant disputes the recourse, I utilize digital communication tools to assess their reasoning. If their reasoning is valid, I close the case electronically with no further action. If their argument is unfounded, I create a digital case summary and forward it electronically to a collection agency that prioritizes low-emission transport options.

As the deadline for any disputes approaches and if no payment has been received, I again utilize digital tools to escalate the case to the collection agency. All documentation involved is maintained electronically to reduce waste, and I ensure that all software used adheres to green principles, prioritizing energy efficiency and the use of renewable resources.
