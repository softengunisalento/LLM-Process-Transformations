If a potential subrogation case is identified, I gather information electronically through secure online databases, reducing the need for physical documentation. I assess the case's viability digitally. If viable, I create and send a digital request for payment to the insurant via email, eliminating paper waste and using low-energy servers for communication. I set an electronic reminder for follow-up, ensuring efficient time management while reducing energy consumption. Should recourse not be possible, I close the case digitally, recording all actions in an environmentally conscious manner.

Upon receipt of payment, I process the transaction using a digital accounting system that relies on renewable energy sources to significantly reduce energy use, and then I close the case electronically. If the insurant disputes the recourse, I review their reasoning through a digital portal to minimize physical interactions and potential emissions associated with travel. If the insurant's position is valid, I close the case within the digital system. If it is deemed incorrect, I digitally forward the case to a collection agency while ensuring the collection agency also utilizes green practices, such as using low-emission vehicles for any necessary transport.

If the deadline for dispute resolution arrives without receipt of payment, I further streamline the process by digitally forwarding the case to the collection agency, ensuring that all documentation is submitted electronically to minimize paper usage and waste. Throughout the process, I focus on optimizing my software tools for energy efficiency and sustainability, aligning with green software principles to reduce the overall carbon footprint of the operations.
