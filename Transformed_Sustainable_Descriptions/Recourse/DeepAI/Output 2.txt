If a potential subrogation case arises, I will utilize an online platform to gather information digitally regarding the insurant. I will verify the details of the case through cloud-based software, which minimizes energy use. If the possibility of recourse is confirmed, I will digitally generate and send a payment request to the insurant via email, which reduces paper usage and transportation emissions. I will set a digital reminder in my calendar application to follow up on this request. If recourse is determined not to be possible, I will document the case and close it in the system, ensuring no physical documentation is wasted.

Upon receipt of the payment, I will record the transaction electronically in our accounting software and officially close the case in the digital system. If the insurant disputes the recourse, I will evaluate their reasoning through a digital communication channel, such as email or an online meeting. If the insurant is correct, I will update the case status in the system and close it without any further action. If their reasoning is not valid, I will submit the case electronically to a collection agency, ensuring that all communications are conducted online to minimize carbon emissions from physical transport.

If the deadline for disagreement passes and no payment has been received, I will again utilize digital channels to forward the case to the collection agency. Throughout this process, I will ensure that any electronic waste generated from device usages, such as old computers or phones, is recycled appropriately. I will also promote the use of renewable energy sources for our office operations to further reduce our carbon footprint and support sustainability. Digital documentation will be employed throughout the process to eliminate production waste and enhance efficiency, ultimately focusing on green software solutions.
