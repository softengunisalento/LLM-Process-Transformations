If an insurant could be possibly subrogated against, I gather information through digital channels to minimize resource use. I verify the case using eco-friendly software tools designed for efficiency. If the possibility for recourse is confirmed, I send the payment request electronically to the insurant, ensuring that the communication is carbon-neutral. I set a digital reminder in my calendar system, utilizing renewable energy for my electricity needs. If recourse is not possible, I promptly close the case in the digital system, ensuring all data is archived in an energy-efficient manner.

When we receive the payment, I process the transaction using electronic banking to reduce paper waste and close the case digitally. If the insurant disputes the recourse, I analyze the reasoning through virtual meetings or secure messaging platforms, avoiding unnecessary travel. If their reasoning is valid, I close the case digitally; if deemed incorrect, I prepare all documentation electronically and forward the case to a collection agency specializing in sustainable practices.

In cases where the deadline for disagreement is reached and no payment has been received, I electronically forward the case to the collection agency as well, ensuring that all actions are documented and handled with minimal energy use and a focus on reducing carbon emissions. Throughout this process, I utilize software designed with green principles in mind to optimize energy efficiency and reduce electronic waste.
