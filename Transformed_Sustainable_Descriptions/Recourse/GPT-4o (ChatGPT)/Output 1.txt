If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I get the necessary information digitally from the available database. I check that case electronically, and if the possibility is confirmed, I send a request for payment to the insurant via email or a secure online platform, eliminating the need for paper or physical mail. I also set up an automated digital reminder to follow up on the case. If recourse is not possible, I close the case in the system and archive it electronically to avoid paper waste.

When we receive the payment, I make a booking electronically in the accounting system and close the case using the same digital platform. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I check the reasoning provided, which is submitted electronically. If the insurant's argument is valid, I close the case directly in the system. If the insurant is wrong, I forward the case digitally to a collection agency, using green software solutions to minimize the energy consumption associated with data processing.

If the deadline for disagreement passes without receiving the payment, the case is forwarded digitally to the collection agency. The entire process runs on servers powered by renewable energy, and cloud services are optimized for energy efficiency. All interactions are paperless, and any necessary physical delivery of documents is done using carbon-neutral shipping methods, prioritizing electric or low-emission vehicles. Waste materials, if any, are recycled, and production waste is avoided through the complete digitization of the workflow.
