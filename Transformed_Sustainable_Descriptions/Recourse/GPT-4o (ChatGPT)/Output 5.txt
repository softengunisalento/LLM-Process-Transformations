I check that case using energy-efficient systems powered by renewable energy sources like solar or wind power. If the possibility of subrogation is confirmed, I send a request for payment to the insurant via email or another secure digital communication platform to minimize paper use and transportation emissions. I set a digital reminder to track the case, using green software designed to optimize energy consumption.

If recourse is not possible, I close the case electronically, reducing unnecessary energy use by ensuring servers and computers are operating on energy-saving modes or powered down when idle. When we receive the money, I record the transaction in a digital accounting system powered by green energy, and the case is closed electronically without generating paper waste. 

If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review their reasoning using energy-efficient digital tools and systems. If their reasoning is valid, I close the case digitally. If their reasoning is incorrect, I forward the case to a collection agency through electronic means, ensuring that all communication is conducted via email or secure cloud platforms to minimize physical transportation and paper use.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and no payment has been received, I forward the case to the collection agency digitally.
