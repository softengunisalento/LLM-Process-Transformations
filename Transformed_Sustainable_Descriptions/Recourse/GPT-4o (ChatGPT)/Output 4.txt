If an insurant could possibly be subrogated against, I retrieve the necessary information digitally, using an energy-efficient database or cloud service powered by renewable energy. I evaluate the case using green software, optimized for low energy consumption, and if the possibility of recourse exists, I send a payment request to the insurant via secure email or another digital communication platform to avoid paper waste and reduce emissions from physical delivery. I also set an automated digital reminder, which runs on energy-efficient systems, instead of a manual or paper-based system. If recourse is not possible, I close the case digitally, avoiding the generation of unnecessary files or paper.

When we receive the payment, I perform the booking in a digital accounting system, reducing paper use, and close the case electronically, ensuring no waste is generated. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I evaluate their reasoning using energy-efficient software and systems. If they are correct, I close the case electronically, minimizing waste and energy consumption. If they are wrong, I forward the case to a collection agency digitally, avoiding any physical transportation or emissions from traditional mailing services.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and no payment has been received, I automatically forward the case to a collection agency electronically, using low-emission or electric vehicles for any necessary physical handling by the collection agency, but prioritizing digital processes to reduce carbon footprint. All actions are executed with a focus on energy efficiency, using servers powered by renewable energy, and by optimizing digital storage and communication to minimize energy usage and environmental impact.
