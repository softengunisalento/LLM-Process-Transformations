If an insurant could be possibly subrogated against, I retrieve the necessary information through digital platforms, avoiding the use of paper-based requests. I check the case using energy-efficient software running on servers powered by renewable energy. If the possibility for subrogation is confirmed, I send a request for payment to the insurant electronically (via email or secure online portal) to avoid physical mailing and reduce carbon emissions. I set a digital reminder in a cloud-based system powered by renewable energy sources, reducing the need for paper-based notes or reminders.

If recourse is not possible, I close the case in the digital system, ensuring that no unnecessary data is stored to minimize energy consumption by data servers. When we receive the payment, I make a digital booking in an eco-friendly accounting system and close the case electronically. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review the reasoning using digital tools, avoiding paper waste. If the insurant is correct, I close the case immediately without any further processing. If the insurant is wrong, I forward the case to a collection agency digitally, avoiding paper documentation and using only agencies that commit to sustainable practices, such as operating electric vehicles and using green energy in their operations.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and no payment has been received, I forward the case digitally to the collection agency as well, ensuring that all communications remain paperless and energy-efficient. Throughout the process, I ensure that all software and systems used are optimized for energy efficiency, minimizing their carbon footprint, and powered by renewable energy. Any hardware involved is properly recycled at the end of its lifecycle, and unnecessary production waste is avoided by maintaining fully digital workflows.
