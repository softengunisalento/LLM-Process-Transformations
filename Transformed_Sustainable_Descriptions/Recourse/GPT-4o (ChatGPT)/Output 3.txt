If an insurant could be possibly subrogated against, I obtain the necessary information digitally through an electronic system, avoiding physical documents or in-person meetings to reduce carbon emissions. I check the case using cloud-based systems powered by renewable energy, ensuring low energy consumption. If the possibility for subrogation is valid, I send a request for payment via email or secure online communication tools, reducing the need for paper or physical transport. I set a digital reminder in the system, eliminating the need for manual reminders.

If recourse is not possible, I close the case digitally, archiving it in an eco-friendly way without the use of physical storage or printouts. When we receive the payment, I process the booking using green software that is energy efficient, and I close the case electronically. If the insurant disagrees with the recourse, I review the reasoning digitally, again avoiding physical paperwork. If the insurant's objection is valid, I close the case electronically. If the objection is not valid, I forward the case to a collection agency using digital communication methods, reducing the need for physical intervention.

If the deadline for disagreement is reached and no payment has been received, I forward the case electronically to a collection agency, avoiding the use of paper, transportation, or energy-intensive processes. Throughout the process, all systems and communications use renewable energy, and data storage is optimized for efficiency to minimize energy consumption.
