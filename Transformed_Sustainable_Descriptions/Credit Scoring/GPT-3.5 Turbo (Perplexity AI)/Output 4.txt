The sales clerks in a bank can utilize their software frontend to digitally receive credit-scoring for customers, initiating a streamlined process within the banking system that communicates electronically with the scoring agency. This process sends a scoring request to the agency immediately upon initiation, eliminating the need for physical documentation or manual interventions. The agency performs an initial quick scoring (level 1) through automated systems, which typically results in an immediate digital response sent back to the banking system within seconds. The banking process then presents this result directly to the clerk on the frontend, minimizing energy use by avoiding unnecessary physical interactions.

In instances where immediate scoring is not possible, the agency informs the banking system of the delay through automated notifications, thus maintaining transparency without requiring physical communication. The agency then proceeds with a level 2 scoring, which is executed through efficient digital processes that may take a few minutes. Once this scoring result is finalized, it is transmitted back to the banking system electronically. The banking process updates the clerk's frontend with a message indicating that they should check back later for results, ensuring that clerks remain informed without wasting resources on follow-up inquiries.

To further enhance sustainability, all actions are powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind power, reducing reliance on fossil fuels. Additionally, any waste generated from the process is minimized and recycled where possible, adhering to circular economy principles. The approach emphasizes green software practices by optimizing code efficiency and reducing server load during operations, contributing to lower energy consumption overall. By digitizing these processes and focusing on eco-friendly practices, the bank not only enhances operational efficiency but also significantly reduces its carbon footprint while fostering a culture of sustainability.
