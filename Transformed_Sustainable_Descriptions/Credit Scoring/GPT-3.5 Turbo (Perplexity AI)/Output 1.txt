The sales clerks in a bank can utilize an enhanced software frontend to digitally receive credit-scoring for customers, initiating a streamlined and eco-friendly process within the banking system that communicates directly with the agency. This process begins with an electronic scoring request sent to the agency, minimizing the need for physical documentation and reducing paper waste. The agency conducts an initial quick scoring (level 1) using energy-efficient servers, which often yields immediate results returned to the banking system within seconds. This digital exchange reduces energy consumption associated with traditional communication methods.

In cases where immediate scoring is not possible, the agency informs the banking process of the delay through a digital notification, thus eliminating unnecessary follow-up calls or paper notices. The agency then initiates level 2 scoring, which is optimized to run during periods of low carbon intensity in the energy grid, ensuring that energy use is minimized and sourced from renewable options whenever feasible. After determining the scoring result, this information is sent back electronically to the banking process.

The banking system then updates the clerk's frontend with the results without requiring any physical interaction. In instances of delays, a digital message prompts the clerk to check back later, further reducing unnecessary energy use and promoting efficient time management. By implementing these digital processes and focusing on renewable energy sources, the bank not only enhances operational efficiency but also significantly reduces its carbon footprint while promoting sustainable practices in its workflow.
