The sales clerks in a bank can use their software frontend to receive the credit-scoring for a certain customer. This starts a process in the banking system which communicates with the agency in the background. This process sends a scoring request to the agency right after the beginning. The request is sent digitally to minimize paper use and reduce carbon footprint.

The agency performs a first quick scoring (level 1) using energy-efficient servers powered by renewable energy sources. This will often lead to an immediate result which is then returned directly to the banking system within seconds. The banking process presents the result to the clerk sitting at the frontend.

Sometimes the scoring cannot be determined immediately and takes longer. In this case, the agency informs the banking process of the delay digitally and then starts the level 2 scoring (which can take up to a couple of minutes). The level 2 scoring also utilizes energy-efficient servers and green software practices to minimize energy consumption. After the scoring result is determined, the information is sent back to the banking process digitally. The banking process displays a message to the clerk when they receive information about the delay to check again later. As soon as the result arrives, it can be seen at the frontend.

Throughout the process, all communications and data transfers are handled electronically to reduce the need for physical documents and transportation, thereby minimizing the carbon footprint. The use of digital processes and green software ensures that the entire operation is as sustainable and environmentally friendly as possible.
