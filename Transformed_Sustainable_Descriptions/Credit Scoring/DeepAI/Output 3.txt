In a sustainable banking process, the sales clerks in a bank utilize an eco-friendly software frontend, designed with green software principles to minimize energy consumption during operation. Upon initiating a credit-scoring request for a customer, the banking system communicates electronically with the scoring agency through a secure and optimized connection, reducing data transmission energy use. The system sends a scoring request to the agency immediately after initiation. The agency then conducts a swift level 1 scoring using algorithms that ensure efficiency and low energy expenditure. This scoring process frequently results in an immediate output, transmitted back to the banking system instantaneously, thus allowing the clerk to view the result promptly on the frontend. In cases where an immediate scoring result cannot be provided, the agency notifies the banking process digitally about the delay via automated messaging, avoiding any unnecessary paper communication. This initiates the level 2 scoring procedure, which is designed to consume minimal resources and runs on renewable energy sources where possible, taking up to a few minutes to complete. Once the deeper scoring result is reached, it is sent back to the banking process electronically, facilitating a rapid response. Meanwhile, the banking system also prompts the clerk through a digital notification to check back later if the scoring is still pending, ensuring effective communication without generating paper waste. As soon as the final result is available, it appears on the frontend, allowing for an organized and efficient follow-up without contributing to environmental waste. Throughout the process, efforts to recycle any generated digital waste, such as data manually entered or processed but not needed, are implemented, contributing to a reduced carbon footprint and an overall sustainable banking operation.
