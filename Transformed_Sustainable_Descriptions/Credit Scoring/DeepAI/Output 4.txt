The sales clerks in a bank can utilize an eco-friendly software frontend designed with energy-efficient coding practices to receive instantaneous digital credit-scoring for customers. This initiates an efficient process in the banking system that leverages cloud computing powered by renewable energy sources. Instead of traditional communication methods, the banking system directly interfaces with the agency via low-energy, encrypted digital channels.

Upon initiation, the banking process digitally transmits a scoring request to the agency. The agency conducts an initial quick scoring (level 1), utilizing advanced algorithms that optimize processing time. This scoring often yields an immediate result, which is transmitted back to the banking system within seconds through energy-efficient data transfer methods. The banking process then presents this result directly on the clerk’s digital interface.

In instances where immediate scoring is not feasible, the agency notifies the banking process of a processing delay via automated digital alerts. The agency subsequently begins the level 2 scoring, which is optimized for low energy consumption and can take several minutes. Once the final scoring result is computed, it is sent back to the banking system sustainably, minimizing data transfer energy costs. 

Upon receiving the update regarding the delay, the banking process intelligently prompts the clerk through the digital interface to check back later, thus reducing the need for unnecessary manual follow-ups that could result in energy waste. When the final result arrives, it instantly updates the frontend application, allowing for real-time access without requiring any physical documentation or on-site interaction, thereby reducing paper waste and reinforcing a commitment to sustainability while promoting green software development practices throughout the process.
