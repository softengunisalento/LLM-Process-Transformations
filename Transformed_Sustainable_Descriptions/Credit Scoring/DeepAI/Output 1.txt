The sales clerks in a bank utilize a sustainable software frontend to receive ecologically optimized credit-scoring for each customer. This triggers an efficient process within the banking system that communicates with the agency in an energy-efficient manner. The process digitally sends a scoring request to the agency immediately after initiation, utilizing secure electronic communication to minimize paper use and resource consumption. The agency performs an initial quick scoring (level 1) in an optimized digital environment, which typically results in an immediate score that is transmitted back to the banking system within seconds through a low-energy data transfer protocol. The banking system then presents this scoring result on the clerk's frontend, utilizing energy-efficient display technology to reduce power consumption.

In scenarios where the scoring cannot be determined immediately and requires additional time, the agency informs the banking process of the delay digitally, triggering an automatic notification system while minimizing resource usage. Subsequently, the agency initiates level 2 scoring, which is conducted through a cloud-based analysis platform powered by renewable energy sources. This process is designed to limit energy consumption and avoid unnecessary production waste. The scoring at this level may take several minutes, but when the final result is available, it is sent back to the banking process using low-impact data transmission. The banking process then digitally alerts the clerk about the delay, encouraging them to check back later without additional resource use. Once the result becomes available, it is displayed on the frontend in real-time, ensuring an eco-friendly approach throughout the entire credit-scoring process while promoting green software principles to minimize the overall carbon footprint.
