The digital sales clerks in a bank can utilize an eco-friendly software frontend to efficiently receive the credit scoring for a specific customer. This initiation process in the banking system operates in a cloud-based environment powered by renewable energy sources, significantly reducing energy consumption. The banking system communicates electronically with the agency in the background, ensuring minimal carbon emissions. Upon starting the process, a digital scoring request is sent to the agency without the need for physical documents, reducing paper waste.

The agency performs an initial quick scoring (level 1) utilizing advanced algorithms that optimize processing speed, often yielding real-time results returned to the banking system within seconds. This swift electronic communication not only enhances efficiency but also minimizes energy usage, as the system aggregates several requests, thereby reducing server load. The banking process then presents the result to the clerk on the sustainable software interface.

In instances where immediate scoring is not feasible, the agency promptly informs the banking process about the delay through a digital notification system. Subsequently, a level 2 scoring is initiated, which, while it may take several minutes, utilizes energy-efficient data centers powered by renewable energy. Upon completion of the scoring process, the result is transmitted back to the banking system digitally, thus avoiding any unnecessary physical movement.

For cases requiring follow-up, the banking process displays a message to the clerk, advising them to check back later, thereby eliminating repetitive queries that could waste energy. Once the scoring result is finalized, it is delivered directly to the frontend, allowing the clerk to access the outcome through their electronic interface without the delay or environmental impact of manual communications. By fully integrating sustainable practices into the credit scoring process, the bank reinforces its commitment to reducing its carbon footprint while enhancing operational efficiency through digital solutions.
