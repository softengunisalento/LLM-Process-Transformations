The sales clerks in a bank utilize a digital software frontend designed with energy-efficient programming practices to receive the credit scoring for a customer. This action initiates a streamlined process within the banking system that communicates wirelessly with the scoring agency, minimizing physical resource use. Immediately upon starting the process, a digital scoring request is transmitted via secure, low-bandwidth communication to the agency, reducing energy consumption compared to traditional methods. 

The agency performs a rapid, level 1 scoring assessment, leveraging cloud computing resources powered by renewable energy sources. This quick analysis typically yields an immediate result, which is sent back to the banking system within seconds using optimized data transfer protocols. The banking system then present this result directly to the clerk at the digital frontend.

In cases where the scoring cannot be determined instantly and requires more time, the agency sends a proactive digital notification regarding the delay rather than utilizing physical communication methods. The agency then initiates a level 2 scoring analysis, designed to be energy-efficient, which may take a few minutes to complete. Once the score is finalized, the information is transmitted back to the banking process using energy-efficient communication channels.

Upon receiving information about the delay, the banking process updates the digital frontend by displaying a message to the clerk, advising them to check again later without unnecessary resource use. When the final scoring result arrives, it is instantly available for the clerk to view on the digital platform, ensuring a paperless workflow and minimizing production waste. Throughout this entire process, all software components are developed with green coding practices to enhance performance while reducing the carbon footprint, and the entire operation prioritizes digital communication to minimize energy use and material waste.
