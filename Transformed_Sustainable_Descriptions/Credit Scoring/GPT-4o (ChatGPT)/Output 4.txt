The sales clerks in a bank can use their software frontend, which has been optimized for energy efficiency and runs on servers powered by renewable energy sources, to receive the credit-scoring for a certain customer. This starts a process in the banking system, which communicates digitally with the agency in the background, eliminating the need for physical documents and reducing transportation emissions.

The process sends a scoring request to the agency at the beginning, leveraging green communication protocols to minimize energy usage during data transfer. The agency, using low-power and green cloud computing infrastructures, performs a first quick scoring (level 1). This initial scoring often leads to an immediate result, which is then sent back to the banking system within seconds via energy-efficient digital channels. The banking process displays the result directly to the clerk sitting at the frontend, reducing the need for printing or paper usage.

In cases where the scoring cannot be determined immediately and takes longer, the agency informs the banking process digitally, ensuring real-time updates without unnecessary data traffic, and then starts the level 2 scoring, which can take up to a couple of minutes. The level 2 scoring is also performed on green, low-emission computing systems to minimize the environmental impact of extended processing times. Once the result is determined, the information is sent back to the banking process using secure and low-energy data transfer methods. The banking system notifies the clerk digitally about the delay and suggests checking the result later through the software interface, thus avoiding the use of energy-intensive notifications or alerts. When the final result is available, it can be seen immediately at the frontend, maintaining a seamless and low-carbon process flow.

This sustainable version of the process emphasizes energy efficiency, renewable energy sources, digitalization, and the reduction of carbon emissions at every stage, ensuring minimal waste and environmental impact.
