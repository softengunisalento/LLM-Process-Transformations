The sales clerks in a bank can use their energy-efficient software frontend, optimized for low energy consumption, to receive the credit-scoring for a certain customer. This starts a process in the banking system that operates using renewable energy sources, communicating with the agency in the background in a low-impact, cloud-based infrastructure to minimize energy use. 

This process sends a digital scoring request to the agency immediately after initiation, using optimized, energy-efficient communication protocols to reduce data transmission overhead. The agency conducts a first quick scoring (level 1), relying on green software solutions that prioritize low energy consumption and efficient processing. This will often result in an immediate outcome, which is then digitally returned to the banking system within seconds, minimizing the need for any additional hardware usage. The banking process presents the result to the clerk on the frontend through cloud-based, low-energy web services.

In some cases, when the scoring cannot be determined immediately and takes longer, the agency informs the banking process of the delay using low-bandwidth communication to minimize data transfer impact. The agency then begins the level 2 scoring, which may take up to a couple of minutes but uses energy-efficient algorithms and is powered by renewable energy sources. Throughout this process, no physical resources are wasted, as all actions are conducted digitally without any paper-based operations.

When the delay occurs, the banking process displays a message to the clerk on the energy-optimized frontend, advising them to check back later without causing unnecessary server load. Once the result is ready, the information is transmitted back to the banking process using the same low-impact, digital methods. The clerk can view the final scoring result directly on the sustainable, low-energy interface at the frontend, reducing the carbon footprint and ensuring an eco-friendly, digitalized process from start to finish.
