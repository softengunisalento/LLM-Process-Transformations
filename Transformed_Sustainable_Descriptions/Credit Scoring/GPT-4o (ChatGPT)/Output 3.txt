The sales clerks in a bank can use their energy-efficient software frontend, which is designed with green software principles, to receive the credit-scoring for a certain customer. This initiates a process in the banking system that communicates digitally with the agency in the background using low-energy servers powered by renewable energy sources.

The process sends a digital scoring request to the agency using energy-efficient cloud-based communication protocols, minimizing data transmission and processing energy consumption. Immediately after the request, the agency performs a level 1 quick scoring using optimized algorithms that minimize computational load. In many cases, this leads to an immediate result, which is sent directly to the banking system within seconds via eco-friendly digital channels, reducing the need for physical infrastructure and paper waste. The result is displayed on the low-energy consumption frontend used by the clerk.

In cases where the scoring requires additional time, the agency digitally informs the banking process of the delay. The level 2 scoring, which may take longer, is performed with resource-optimized computational methods to minimize energy use. The agency utilizes servers located in energy-efficient, carbon-neutral data centers. Once the level 2 scoring result is ready, it is sent back through the same green communication methods to the banking process. The bank’s system, running on green servers, displays a notification to the clerk to check the scoring status later.

Throughout the process, all communication is handled digitally, eliminating the need for physical documents or travel, thus reducing the carbon footprint. The entire workflow operates on an energy-optimized platform, powered by renewable energy, to ensure minimal environmental impact. Additionally, any hardware involved in the process is part of a recycling program, ensuring electronic waste is minimized.
